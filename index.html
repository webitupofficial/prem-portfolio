About

I like the whole arc of a product: finding the problem, naming the company, writing the code and putting it in front of real users.

I'm based in Jammu. Through Web It Up 24 I build websites and web apps for startups and independent professionals. WebCompliance.in grew out of that work: Indian businesses needed a straightforward way to see where they stand under the DPDP Act, so I built one.

Alongside those, I co-founded Lexminds, a home for young legal writers and interns, and Kaizen, which brings entrepreneurship and innovation programmes to students.
