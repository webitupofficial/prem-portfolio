About

“Statutory law can be refactored like code, and code can be regulated like law.”

I started writing code in 2022, before I'd thought seriously about law. By the time I joined the University of Jammu in 2025, I'd built and hosted a handful of sites and moved a few of them onto Google Cloud.

Law school changed what I build. WebCompliance.in turns the DPDP Act into checks a business can actually run, and Lexminds gives law students somewhere to publish and find internships.

The areas I care most about are technology law, data protection and policy. Away from both, I play chess.
