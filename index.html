A law student who ships software.

01Founder · Legal-tech

WebCompliance.in

Software that helps Indian startups get ready for the Digital Personal Data Protection Act. A free website check runs a real-browser Lighthouse scan and finds privacy and policy pages, while on-site gap audits are delivered with CA and compliance firms and reviewed by a person before the report goes out.

  • TypeScript
  • Upstash Redis
  • Playwright
  • Lighthouse
  • Resend
